Welcome to my Maze Project! In this study, I explored the fascinating world of procedural generation by implementing and analyzing 5 distinct maze generation algorithms. 

The project is divided into two main parts: a comprehensive research paper detailing the mathematical and programmatic logic behind each approach, and an interactive web game where you can generate and navigate these mazes in real-time.
