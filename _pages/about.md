I am an Applied Mathematician and Data Scientist with a probabilistic background. I hold an M.Sc. in Stochastics and Data Science from the University of Turin.

My core interests are building interpretable and trustworthy models in cases where data is limited or noisy, such as in the banking, weather, mechanical, and biomedical fields. In particular, I have worked with Deep Learning models for the detection of rare events in the biomedical sector.

During my university studies, I also participated in an Erasmus+ Exchange at Ghent University in Belgium (2024-2025).

You can find more information about my journey on the [CV page](/cv/), and my code in the [Projects](/projects/) section and on my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/enricodidoli-source).
